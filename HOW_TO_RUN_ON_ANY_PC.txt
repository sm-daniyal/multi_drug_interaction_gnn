HOW TO RUN ON YOUR LAPTOP (WINDOWS):
------------------------------------
Prerequisite:
1. Make sure Python 3.10, 3.11, or 3.12 is installed on your computer.
   (Download from https://www.python.org/downloads/ if not installed.
    IMPORTANT: Check the box "Add python.exe to PATH" when installing).

FIRST TIME RUNNING:
-------------------
Simply double-click:
    INSTALL_AND_RUN.bat

This will automatically:
1. Create a local Python virtual environment (venv) on your laptop.
2. Install all required dependencies (Torch, Torch-Geometric, Streamlit, etc.).
3. Launch the dashboard automatically in your browser at:
   http://localhost:8501

EVERY NEXT TIME:
----------------
Simply double-click:
    RUN_APP.bat

FEATURES READY OUT OF THE BOX:
------------------------------
- 100% Offline SQLite database with 4,064 indexed drug formulations A-Z.
- Real-time typing autocomplete drug search.
- Pre-trained GATv2 model checkpoint loaded and ready for predictions.
- Hospital Clinical Prescription Audit PDF generator with academic watermark.
- Explainable AI (XAI) attention bar charts and 19,734-node Knowledge Graph.
- Collapsible Patient Clinical Factors & Comorbidities sidebar.
